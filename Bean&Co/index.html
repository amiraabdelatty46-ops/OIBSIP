Our Story

Bean & Co started as a tiny cart with one grinder and a big dream. Today we roast our own beans every week and serve them in a warm, friendly corner of the city.

We believe a good cup of coffee can turn any day around. That's why we use fair-trade beans and brew everything fresh.
